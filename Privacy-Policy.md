**Magic Cube Variants Privacy Policy**

At The Gravity (defined here as “We” or “Our”), the developer of the mobile game Magic Cube Variants (the “Game”), We value your comments, suggestions and feedback. For any feedback, please contact the email-address found at the bottom. Our Privacy Policy sets out how we collect, use and disclose “personal information” and other “non-personal information” that is gathered about you in relation to the Game on Android and iOS.  
By using the Game, you acknowledge and consent to the terms of this privacy policy.

This policy will let you know:
  * What information (often referred to as ‘data’) is collected from you;
  * How your information is used;
  * With whom your information may be shared;
  * How you can access, update, or delete your information.

We will comply with all relevant and applicable legislative requirements, and, in the event of any inconsistency, the legislative requirements will override the provisions of this document.

**User Age Limits**

The Game is not aimed at children under 13 years of age. We do not collect information, nor do we direct interest-based advertising to anyone under the age of 13 years.  
If you believe that We have inadvertently collected information from a person under the age of 13, please contact us at the email address below and the information will be permanently deleted.

**What data do we collect?**

We collect data in three ways.
  * Data collected automatically;
  * Data you provide us; and
  * Data collected by our partners.

**Data collected automatically**

Certain data is collected automatically. This includes
  * Data about your game progress;
  * Data about your device, such as device name and operating system, browser type and language;
  * Data about your use of the Game, such as which puzzles you play, your solve times and scores, and your use of features such as tutorials and puzzle scanning; and
  * Your IP addresses, and mobile device identifiers (such as your device ID, advertising ID, MAC address, IMEI). Please note, there are ways to prevent this, which are outlined at ‘Third Party Service Providers’ below.

**Data you provide us**

If you contact us, we will keep the contact information in order for us to get back to you. This includes your email-address and any content within the email.

**Data we collect from our third-party partners**

To operate the Game we use third party service providers. A list of those parties can be found in this privacy policy. The type of information we might collect from them includes:
  * Data we receive if you link a third-party account with the Game (such as signing in with Google Play Games or Apple Game Center);
  * Demographic data (to determine the coarse location of your IP address)
  * Data from the platforms the Game runs on (Google Play and the Apple App Store); and
  * Data for advertising and analytics purposes.
  * If you choose to log in using Google Play Games Services (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS), we will receive certain profile information to authenticate your account and save your progress. This includes your platform player ID and your chosen username (Gamer Tag / Alias). These identifiers are linked to a Unity Gaming Services player profile, which is what our leaderboards use to identify you.

**Leaderboards and Publicly Visible Data**
The Game features leaderboards. To support these, we use two third-party services:

  * Unity Gaming Services (UGS) is used for player authentication. UGS provides a stable anonymous player ID that identifies you across sessions and, if you link your account, across devices.
  * Supabase is used to store the leaderboard data — specifically, your UGS player ID, your chosen display name, and your scores.

If you choose to participate in the leaderboards, certain information will be publicly visible to other players. This includes your in-game username (or Google Play Games / Apple Game Center username, if linked) and your gameplay scores/statistics. By playing the Game and participating in the leaderboards, you consent to this data being shared with Unity Gaming Services and Supabase and displayed publicly within the app. Supabase hosts our leaderboard database on servers located in Germany. Supabase's privacy policy is at https://supabase.com/privacy.

**Camera and Puzzle Scanning**

The Game includes a scan feature that uses your device's camera to recognise the colours of a physical puzzle you are holding, so that the Game can show you how to solve it. The feature is optional: the camera is only used after you choose to scan a puzzle and grant the camera permission, and it is only active while you are scanning.

All processing of the camera image happens on your device. Each camera frame is analysed in memory and discarded straight away. We do not take, store or upload photos or videos, and no image, video or colour data from the camera is sent to us or to any third party. You can revoke the camera permission at any time in your device's settings; the scan feature will then let you enter the puzzle's colours by hand instead.

To improve the feature, we record through our analytics providers that the scan feature was used, which puzzle it was used for, how many stickers were recognised, and whether a solution was found. This is non-personal usage data of the kind described under 'Collection of other Non-Personal Information' below, and it never includes any camera content.
